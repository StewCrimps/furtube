Furtube Privacy Policy


Last Updated 12 August 2026

This Privacy Notice for StewCrimps Studios ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we process your personal information when you use Furtube — upload, share, and watch animal videos from across the world — at furtu.be, or otherwise engage with us. Questions or concerns can be sent to stewcrimps@gmail.com.

  • 1. What Information Do We Collect?

    • We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register, express interest in our products, participate in activities on the Services, or contact us. This may include:
      • Email addresses
      • Usernames
      • Passwords
      • Username handles
    We do not process sensitive personal information.

  • 2. How Do We Process Your Information?

    • We process your information to:
      • Create and manage your account
      • Deliver the Services you request
      • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
      • Send administrative information about our Services
      • Enable communication between users
      • Protect our Services, including fraud monitoring
      • Identify usage trends to improve the Services
      • Protect an individual's vital interests where necessary

  • 3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On?

    • If you are located in the EU or UK, we rely on: consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations, and vital interests.

    If you are located in Canada, we rely on express or implied consent, along with limited legal exceptions (e.g. fraud prevention, legal proceedings, next-of-kin notification, publicly available information).

  • 4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Information?

    • We may share your information:
      • During business transfers such as a merger, sale, or acquisition
      • When you post content or interact publicly — this may be viewed by all users and made available outside the Services

  • 5. What Is Our Stance on Third-Party Websites?

    • Our Services may link to third-party sites or services not affiliated with us. We are not responsible for their content, privacy practices, or security. Review their policies directly.

  • 6. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

    • We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain security, prevent crashes, save preferences, and support analytics and advertising. You can opt out of tracking used for targeted advertising via Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals, which we honor automatically. See our Cookie Notice at furtu.be/cookies-usage/.

  • 7. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

    • We retain your personal information for as long as you maintain an account with us, unless a longer period is required by law.

  • 8. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

    • We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed 100% secure. Use of the Services is at your own risk.

  • 9. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

    • If you are located in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or Canada, you may have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal information, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and avoid solely automated decision-making. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting stewcrimps@gmail.com.
      • UK residents may complain directly to us or to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, Helpline: 0303 123 1113)
      • EEA/UK residents may also complain to their Member State or UK data protection authority
      • Switzerland residents may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner
    You can review or update your account information by logging into your account settings, or request account termination by contacting us. Terminating your account deactivates and deletes your data from active databases, though some information may be retained to prevent fraud or comply with legal requirements.

  • 10. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

    • We do not currently respond to browser Do-Not-Track (DNT) signals, as no uniform standard exists. We do, however, recognize and honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out request for targeted advertising under applicable state privacy laws, including the CCPA.

  • 11. Do United States Residents Have Specific Privacy Rights?

    • Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, or Virginia may have the right to:
      • Know whether we are processing your personal data
      • Access your personal data
      • Correct inaccuracies
      • Request deletion
      • Obtain a copy of data you've shared with us
      • Non-discrimination for exercising these rights
      • Opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or profiling
    In the past 12 months, we collected identifiers (e.g. email, username) and internet activity data. We have not sold or shared personal information with third parties for commercial purposes, and do not plan to.

    To exercise your rights, contact furtu.be.official@gmail.com or stewcrimps@gmail.com. We honor opt-out preferences enacted via Global Privacy Control. If we decline your request, you may appeal by emailing stewcrimps@gmail.com; if the appeal is denied, you may complain to your state attorney general.

    California residents may also request info about data disclosed for direct marketing purposes once per year, free of charge, under the "Shine the Light" law.

  • 12. Do We Make Updates to This Notice?

    • Yes. We may update this Privacy Notice periodically to stay compliant with applicable law. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Material changes may be communicated via notice on the Services or direct notification.
